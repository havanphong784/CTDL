## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation

Use a bright, simple, modern artistic style for a DSA quiz application. Prefer warm off-white backgrounds, paper-like surfaces, thin ink borders, and restrained accent colors. Use teal as the primary interaction color, with coral and ochre as artistic secondary accents. Avoid dark dashboards, glassmorphism, heavy gradients, and crowded sidebars.

Layout should feel gallery-like and spacious: large typography on the setup page, airy two-column composition, generous whitespace, and clear step hierarchy. Cards should be functional and flat with 8px radius, precise spacing, and elegant linework. Use abstract geometric accents only as small integrated marks, not decorative blobs.

For the quiz page, focus attention on the question. Use one wide content column for the question and answer options, plus one narrow utility column for dictionary terms. Buttons should be clear, calm, and tactile. Option states should be readable: selected teal, correct green, incorrect coral. The visual language must remain lightweight, academic, and modern.

Typography: system sans-serif, strong editorial headings, compact UI labels, no negative letter spacing. Maintain legible Vietnamese text at all breakpoints.
